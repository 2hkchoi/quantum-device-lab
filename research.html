Research

Quantum transport, correlations, and thermodynamics in nanoscale devices.

We use tunable mesoscopic systems to investigate how charge, spin, coherence, entropy, and many-body effects emerge in quantum electronic devices.

Research themes

Our work combines precision low-temperature measurements with device engineering to address fundamental questions in quantum matter.

Quantum Devices

Quantum Dots

Charge, spin, orbital degrees of freedom, interdot coupling, and controllable quantum states in semiconductor nanostructures.

Many-body Physics

Strongly Correlated Systems

Kondo correlations, local moments, hybridization, and emergent many-body behavior in mesoscopic electronic systems.

Entropy & Heat

Quantum Thermodynamics

Entropy, degeneracy, and thermodynamic signatures as probes of microscopic quantum states and correlations.

2D Materials

Graphene-based Devices

Quantum electronic phenomena in graphene and related nanoscale heterostructures.
