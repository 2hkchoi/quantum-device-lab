A useful first email.

A short, specific introduction helps us understand your interests and discuss possible projects.

    Introduce yourself

    Share your current university, degree program, academic background, and the type of opportunity you are considering.

  2. 02

    Tell us what interests you

    Mention a research theme or experimental question that caught your attention. Relevant coursework or experience provides helpful context.

  3. 03

    Discuss a possible fit

    Include your preferred start period and any questions about available projects. A concise CV is useful if you already have one.
