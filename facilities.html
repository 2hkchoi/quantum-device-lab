Facilities

Experimental infrastructure from nanofabrication to quantum measurement.

Our laboratory combines cryogenic measurement systems, device preparation tools, and shared nanofabrication facilities for research on quantum electronic devices.

In our laboratory

QDL Equipment

Core equipment operated directly by the Quantum Device Lab for device preparation, cryogenic measurements, and rapid prototyping.

Dry Dilution Refrigerator

Millikelvin platform for low-temperature quantum transport experiments.

4K Cryocooler

Cryogenic platform for low-temperature device measurements.

2D Transfer Station

Assembly and transfer of van der Waals heterostructures and 2D materials.

Probe Station

Electrical probing and room-temperature characterization of devices.

Wedge Wire Bonder

Electrical interconnection of fabricated devices and chip carriers.

Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM)

High-resolution imaging and inspection of micro- and nanostructures.

3D Printer

Rapid prototyping of laboratory fixtures and custom experimental components.

Collaborative infrastructure

Shared Facilities

Nanofabrication and characterization systems available through shared research facilities and collaborative access.

Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM)

TESCAN system for high-resolution structural characterization.

Thermal Evaporator

Thin-film metal deposition for device fabrication.

Mask Aligner 1

Photolithography alignment and exposure for microfabrication.

Mask Aligner 2

PROLine W150-BA system for aligned photolithography processing.

Stylus Profilometer

Bruker DektakXT for step-height and surface-profile measurements.

Plasma Cleaner

PIE Scientific Tergeo Plus for surface cleaning and plasma treatment.

Sputtering System

Thin-film deposition system for device and material processing.

ICP RIE

Inductively coupled plasma reactive-ion etching for anisotropic pattern transfer.
